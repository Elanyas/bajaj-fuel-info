Privacy Policy

Updated 2026

At NexAlarm, your privacy is our foundation. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how it is used.

1. Information Collection

NexAlarm collects personal information when you sign in via Google. This includes:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Usage Data: We use this information to synchronize your alarm settings, vision board goals, and VAN asset balance across multiple devices securely.

2. App Permissions

To provide its core functionality, NexAlarm requires the following permissions:

  • Exact Alarm: Crucial for ensuring your alarms trigger at the exact millisecond scheduled.
  • Camera: Used strictly for Mission-based alarms (such as Barcode or QR code scanning) to verify tasks are completed.
  • Protection Guard (Accessibility Service): An optional advanced feature used strictly to prevent the app from being force-closed or uninstalled while an alarm is active. No personal activity is monitored or collected.
  • Device Administrator: Used to provide basic uninstall protection.
  • Storage/Media: Used to allow you to select and display custom images and sounds for your Vision Board and alarms.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate reliable third-party services to enhance your experience:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying rewarded advertisements to earn VAN assets.
  • Google Play Billing: For secure processing of in-app purchases.

These services may collect device-specific identifiers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. VAN Assets & Digital Items

NexAlarm uses a virtual currency called "VAN Assets" for unlocking premium missions and features.

  • Non-Refundable: All purchases of VAN Assets or items unlocked using VAN Assets are final and non-refundable once the transaction is completed.
  • In-App Use Only: VAN Assets are virtual tokens with no real-world monetary value. They are intended solely for use within the NexAlarm application and cannot be transferred, sold, or redeemed for cash.
  • Account Bound: Your VAN balance is tied to your Google Account. Deleting your account will result in the permanent loss of all accumulated VAN Assets without compensation.

5. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information at any time.

Account Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, you can do so by navigating to Settings > Delete Account (located at the bottom of the page). This action will immediately and permanently wipe all your records from our database.

6. Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data during transmission and while stored. All account synchronization is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
