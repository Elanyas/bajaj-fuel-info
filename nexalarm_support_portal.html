SUPPORT PORTAL

Expert assistance for your NexAlarm experience

How do I ensure my alarms ring on time?
Navigate to 'Alarm Optimization' in Settings. It is crucial to disable system battery optimization for NexAlarm to ensure the Android system doesn't suspend the app's background tasks.
Can I customize sound and vibration?
Yes. Open 'Alarm Setting' to choose from a variety of sounds, adjust vibration patterns, and enable 'Gentle Wake-up' which gradually increases volume for a peaceful start.
What are Dismiss Missions?
Missions are mental or physical challenges (like Math, Memory, or Barcode scans) required to stop the alarm. Configure them in 'Dismiss Alarm/Mission' to prevent oversleeping.
How do I set my Life Goals?
Go to 'Life Goal Setting' to personalize your morning vision board. Your top goals and quotes will appear immediately after you wake up to provide instant motivation.
What does Premium Membership offer?
Premium status removes all advertisements, unlocks advanced security modules, and provides access to exclusive morning themes and advanced mission types.
How can I earn free VAN assets?
You can earn VAN by watching rewarded ads in the 'Get VAN' screen. VAN assets can be used to unlock premium features without a subscription.
Does the app support custom themes?
Absolutely. While NexAlarm features a signature dark theme, you can explore further visual customizations under 'General' -> 'Theme Selection'.
How can I back up my settings?
Cloud backup and local export options are available in 'General Settings'. This ensures your alarms and life goals are safe if you change devices.
Is my data private?
Yes. We prioritize your privacy. Only essential data is stored locally or securely synced. View our full 'Privacy Policy' in the Settings menu for details.
How do I delete my account?
If you wish to remove your account and all associated data permanently, select 'Delete Account' at the bottom of the Settings page. This action is irreversible.
